# Privacy Policy for Planet Sort

**Effective date:** September 26, 2026  
**Developer:** Teleferic  
**Contact:** veznedaroglufayik2@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy describes how Teleferic ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Planet Sort mobile game (the "App").

## Information We Handle

### Information stored on your device

The App stores your current level and whether you have purchased the ad-free option locally on your device. This information is used to save your progress and apply your purchase. The App does not provide an account or profile feature, and this locally stored game data is not sent to a Teleferic server.

### Advertising

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to provide banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. When ads are requested or displayed, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or receive information such as your IP address, device or advertising identifiers, device and operating system details, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information. An approximate location may be inferred from your IP address. The information used can depend on your device settings, consent choices, and the advertising configuration.

Google processes this information under its own privacy policy: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### In-app purchases

The App offers a one-time purchase to remove ads. Purchases are initiated through Google Play using Unity In-App Purchasing. Google Play and the services involved in processing the purchase may handle purchase and transaction information, such as the purchased product and transaction or receipt details, to complete and validate the transaction. We use the purchase result to enable the ad-free option. Payment card details are handled by Google Play, not collected by the App.

For more information, see [Google Play's Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/) and [Unity's Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## How Information Is Used

Information handled by the App and its service providers is used to:

- Save game progress and remember the ad-free purchase on your device.
- Deliver and measure advertisements, including rewarded ads you choose to view.
- Process and validate in-app purchases.
- Maintain, protect, and troubleshoot the App and the services it uses.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Google, Google Play, Unity, and their service providers as needed to provide advertising, billing, app functionality, and related services. Those providers handle information under their own privacy terms and policies. The App does not currently use a Teleferic-operated account system or game backend.

## Retention and Deletion

Game progress and the local ad-free purchase flag remain on your device until you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App. Clearing local data does not delete purchase records held by Google Play. Google, Unity, and advertising partners retain information they process according to their own policies and retention practices.

## Your Choices

- You can clear the App's locally stored game data through your device settings or by uninstalling the App.
- You can manage advertising and privacy settings through your device and Google account settings, where available.
- You can manage purchase records and applicable refunds through Google Play.
- For questions or privacy requests concerning information handled by Teleferic, contact us at the email address above. Requests about data held by Google, Unity, or another provider may need to be directed to that provider.

## Children

The App does not offer account registration and does not ask users to submit personal information directly. The App does use third-party advertising services, which may process device and advertising information as described above. The age groups selected for the App in Google Play and the advertising settings must accurately reflect its intended audience. Where children may use the App, advertising and data handling must also comply with applicable children's privacy laws and Google Play's Families policies.

## Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by us. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Information handled by third-party providers is also subject to their security practices.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its services, or applicable requirements change. We will post the updated policy and revise the effective date above.
